# Confirmed conversation price — strict amendment suggestion

SamePage does not treat a chat reply such as "I agree" as formal agreement approval.

When the saved Meta conversation review contains source-linked observations of
(1) a specific total-price condition from one participant and (2) the other
participant's immediate, unqualified confirmation, the application can display
a draft *price-only* amendment, without making another Meta API request.

This only works when the quotes are present in the authenticated conversation,
the currencies match the approved price, only one price amount is mentioned,
the people are different and the review is current. Other terms remain unchanged.
The draft's initiating author must actively click **Send for approval**; the
other participant must approve the proposed amendment. The activity history
retains the source price message and the confirming message. Questions, vague
or qualified acceptance, missing quotes and ambiguous amounts are not recovered.

Delivery screenshots are a separate stage: first send a checklist, have the
other person approve it, then the responsible participant submits the work and
up to two screenshots with the delivery statement. The reviewer optionally
requests Meta's image insight. The reviewer—not Meta—accepts or disputes the
work. Attached screenshots alone do not prove authenticity or functionality.

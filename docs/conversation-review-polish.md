# Conversation review polish

- A quoted total **amount should be $700** followed by the other participant’s direct **I agree** is eligible to **prepare**, never apply, a price amendment. The original $600 remains until both formal approvals.
- Saved review evidence is reused when refreshing; Meta is not called again to prepare.
- The conversation review action shows a spinner and truthful explanatory text while a request is running; no fabricated percentage or artificial staged progress is shown.
- The model is never permitted to approve terms. Unquoted, ambiguous, stale, withdrawn or conflicting statements remain non-actionable.
